How the pipeline works

  1. Upload a case. Four co-registered sequences (t1, t1ce, t2, flair) as a .zip of BraTS-style .nii/.nii.gz files, or one 4-D .nii/.nii.gz.
  2. Image quality. Seven quality metrics (SNR, bias field, motion artefacts …) are computed from the FLAIR volume — the same metrics that steer the model at inference time.
  3. Three experts predict the findings. A radiomics model on texture features, a CNN and a ViT, each reading 16 cached slices of the scan. Five structured findings are predicted (edema, enhancing tumor, necrosis …).
  4. Routing and gating. A quality router combines the three experts according to this scan's quality profile; a confidence gate then decides whether the case is released or deferred for review.
  5. Grounded report. Every sentence in the report is tied to the evidence that produced it (a probability, a quality metric, or the gate decision). Optional VLM phrases are vetted by veto rules before they may appear.
